Overview

About the Atlas Garage Pro 8000 (PH8S)

This is the lift home garages have been waiting for. The PH8S is Atlas's portable hobbyist four-post: an 8,000 lb drive-on platform that lets you park one vehicle over another and instantly double your garage. With 67½" of clearance under the runways, there's genuine room for the car below — a daily driver under the summer car, or a project under the keeper.

It's just as capable as a service lift. Raise a vehicle to a comfortable height and you have honest access for brakes, exhaust, oil changes, and detailing, with the included jack tray ready for wheels-free work. Atlas's lock-ladder column design engages a mechanical lock at every rung, and four plastic drip trays keep whatever's parked underneath clean. Steel approach ramps, wheel stops, and rubber wheel chocks are all in the box; add the optional caster kit and the whole lift rolls to where you need it.

The part homeowners appreciate most: it runs on standard 110V power with a 30-amp breaker — no 220V rewire, no electrician's invoice before the first lift. We've been installing a lot of these across Vancouver Island lately, including a recent install near Miracle Beach where a collector now stacks two classic Chevys in a single bay — read the story on Shop News. Every PH8S from SVI is delivered, professionally installed, and load-tested before handover.
