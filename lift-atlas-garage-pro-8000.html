Overview

About the Atlas Garage Pro 8000

It uses the same lock ladder column system found on professional lifts, with a double-lock safety system and a manual single-point release that frees all four locks at once. Flow restrictor valves and zerk fittings at each corner keep it running smoothly.

It doesn't need to be anchored to the floor, and the included polyurethane caster kit lets you roll it across an epoxy floor without marking it. Steel approach ramps, three drip trays, steel jack trays and rubber wheel chocks are all included. Anchor bolts are optional if you plan to use it many times a day.

Warranty Information

Every Atlas lift supplied and installed by SVI Automotive Equipment is covered by the Atlas manufacturer warranty and backed by our local service across Vancouver Island.

Standard Manufacturer Warranty

  • 1 year on power units, cylinders and major components
