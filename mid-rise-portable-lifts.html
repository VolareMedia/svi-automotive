FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a mid-rise lift?

A mid-rise lift raises a vehicle to roughly waist or chest height, around 40", instead of full standing height. That's ideal for wheels, brakes and suspension, and it works under lower ceilings.

Do portable lifts need to be anchored?

Both the Atlas Kwik Bay and the Atlas Versa Extended are free-standing portable lifts, so no anchoring is required.

What power do they need?

The Atlas Kwik Bay runs on a 120V power unit (30A breaker) plus shop air (85–120 PSI). The Atlas Versa Extended uses a pneumatic (air) pump. See each lift's page for full specs.

Can I finance a portable lift?

Yes. $0-down lease-to-own financing is available through Lease Link Canada, with terms up to 60 months. See financing options.
