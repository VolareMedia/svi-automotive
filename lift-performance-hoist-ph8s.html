Overview

About the Performance Hoist PH8S

This is the lift home garages have been waiting for. The Performance Hoist PH8S is an 8,000 lb drive-on four-post that lets you park one vehicle over another and instantly double your garage. With a full 68" of clearance under the runways, there's genuine room for the car below — a daily driver under the summer car, or a project under the keeper — and it's just as handy for motorcycles, ATVs, yard equipment, and storage totes.

It's a capable service lift too. Raise a vehicle to a comfortable height and you have honest access for brakes, exhaust, oil changes, and detailing, with the included sliding tool/jack tray ready for wheels-free work. Ladder-style locking columns engage a mechanical lock on all four posts and let you fine-tune lock heights to level the runways on a sloped floor, the runways and cross beams are reinforced with welded-on steel doubling, and six drip trays covering the full length of the lift keep whatever's parked underneath clean.

The parts homeowners appreciate most: it runs on standard 110V power with just a 20-amp breaker, and it rolls on included polyurethane wheel castors that won't mark an epoxy floor — anchor bolts are in the box, but bolting down is optional for parking duty. We've been installing a lot of these across Vancouver Island lately, including a recent install near Miracle Beach where a collector now stacks two classic Chevys in a single bay — read the story on Shop News. Every Performance Hoist PH8S from SVI is delivered, professionally installed, and load-tested before handover.
