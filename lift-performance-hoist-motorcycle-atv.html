Overview

About the Performance Hoist Motorcycle/ATV Lift

This is a heavy-duty powersports lift, not a lightweight bike jack. With 2,200 lbs of capacity it handles everything from a sport bike to a big touring motorcycle, and it's just as much at home under an ATV, quad, side-by-side or snowmobile. It's a fit for the home garage where the bikes live, and for the powersports shop that services them all season.

The platform is built in three sections. The main deck is 30" wide, right for a motorcycle, and two 15" slide-in extensions bring it out to a full 60" for four-wheelers. Each extension rides on four pipe slides, so there's no deflection with the wings in. Front and rear drop-out panels let you pull either wheel without taking the machine off the lift, and the front wheel chock sits on a panel that extends even further for extra-long motorcycles.

Lifting is done by a heavy-duty hydraulic cylinder on standard 110V power, so the rise is smooth and steady rather than jerky, and built-in safety locks hold the deck at height. The control box is magnetic: stick it out of the way, grab it when you need it. The whole lift rolls in any direction on its built-in castor kit, and the wide base keeps it planted while you work. It comes with a motorcycle lifting jack, three drive-on approach ramps, and tie-down hooks with straps, so it's ready to work the day it arrives.
