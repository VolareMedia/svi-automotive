The Performance Hoist PH8S Home Garage Boom

The four-post parking lift that's been flying out of our warehouse — and why Island homeowners keep putting them in their garages.

One Bay.

Every so often one piece of equipment starts leaving the warehouse faster than we can restock it. Right now, that's the Performance Hoist PH8S — an 8,000 lb four-post parking lift from an aftermarket brand that's built a real following by delivering serious hardware at a sharp price. Over the past few months we've seen a boom in home garage installs of this lift across Vancouver Island, and once you see one in a garage, you understand why: it's the simplest way ever invented to make one bay hold two cars.

What the Performance Hoist PH8S Actually Does for a Garage

The idea is almost embarrassingly simple. You drive a car onto the runways, raise it overhead, and park a second car underneath. That's it — your garage just doubled. But the follow-on effects are what win people over:

  • More floor space, instantly. The car you drive least lives up in the air. The floor below stays open for your daily driver, your workbench, or the next project.
  • Stack your cars, protect your cars. Seasonal and collector cars stay inside, out of the weather, without renting storage across town. Six drip trays covering the full length of the runways come standard, so the car parked below stays spotless.
  • A real service lift too. With 8,000 lbs of capacity and a 72½" rise, it handles brakes, exhaust, oil changes, and detailing in comfort — and the included sliding tool/jack tray lets you go wheels-free when a job calls for it.
  • Safety built in. Ladder-style locking columns engage a mechanical lock on all four posts — and let you fine-tune lock heights to level the runways on a sloped floor — so the lift sits on solid steel, not just hydraulics, while it's parked.

Built for Home Garages, Not Just Shops

Plenty of lifts can raise a car. What makes the Performance Hoist PH8S such an easy fit at home comes down to three things:

  • It runs on standard 110V power. A 20-amp breaker is all it asks — lighter than most lifts on the market. No 220V line, no panel upgrade, no electrician's invoice before your first lift.
  • It fits real garages. There's a full 68" of clearance under the runways — enough for a second vehicle or a very comfortable place to work — and the runways and cross beams are reinforced with welded-on steel doubling to prevent deflection.
  • It moves when you do. Polyurethane wheel castors come standard — kind to epoxy floors — so the whole lift rolls to a new spot in the garage. Anchor bolts are in the box if you'd rather bolt it down, but for parking duty they're optional.

Drive-on ramps, drip trays, wheel stops, and the tool/jack tray are all included, and the whole package is backed by a 3-year warranty on structure and hydraulics. If you've read our home garage lift guide, you already know the four-post drive-on is the easiest style of lift to live with day to day — the Performance Hoist PH8S is the one Island buyers keep choosing.

A Recent Install Near Miracle Beach

A picture tells this story better than a spec sheet. A collector up near Miracle Beach recently had us install a Performance Hoist PH8S in his home garage, where two classic Chevys now share a single bay — the Chevy II wagon parks up top, and the Chevelle tucks in underneath with room to spare. The lift shares the floor with the two-post he wrenches on, and between the two of them the garage works like a proper shop.

The client couldn't be happier — his words, not ours. And he's not alone: we've delivered and installed several more Performance Hoist PH8S lifts across the Island since that job, from Victoria to the Comox Valley. Every one was the same story — a homeowner who thought they were out of space, and a garage that had a second storey hiding in it all along.

Is the Performance Hoist PH8S Right for Your Garage?

Three quick things to check before you get excited (we'll verify all of them before you buy):

  • Ceiling height. To stack two vehicles comfortably you'll want a taller-than-minimum ceiling — measure to the lowest obstruction, including the garage door track. For lower ceilings, ask us about the right configuration for your space.
  • Floor. A sound concrete slab is all the Performance Hoist PH8S needs for parking duty.
  • Power. A 110V circuit on a 20-amp breaker — most garages already have one, and the upgrade is minor if not.
