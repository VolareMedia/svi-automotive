Overview

About the Atlas OH-10X

Single-piece 12' 7" formed columns and extra-long carriages give maximum column and arm strength. Extra-long direct-drive cylinders lift several inches higher than many lifts, so taller techs can walk under the vehicle, and the symmetric columns leave a wide drive-through for dual-wheel pickups.

A padded automatic shut-off bar protects vehicle roofs, rubber carriage covers help prevent door damage, and a single-point lock release unlocks both columns from one side. The single-piece columns need a ceiling of at least 13 feet, and the power unit needs a 220V, 30A circuit.
