A good motorcycle lift gets the bike up to a comfortable working height. You can service the chain, change tires, swap oil and detail it without kneeling on the garage floor.

Table lifts like the Performance Hoist work the same way for every machine: roll it up the ramps onto the platform, secure the front wheel in the chock (or the optional vise), and raise it. Drop-out panels at the front and rear let you pull either wheel without taking the bike off the lift, and slide-in extensions widen the deck for ATVs, quads and side-by-sides.

Capacity2,200 lbs handles your heaviest touring bike or a side-by-side with room to spare.
Wheel chock and locksA removable front wheel chock (vise optional) and built-in safety locks hold the deck at height.
Footprint87" long and 30" wide with the wings out, and it rolls out of the way on built-in castors.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you sell motorcycle lifts?

Yes. SVI carries the Performance Hoist Motorcycle/ATV Lift, a heavy-duty 2,200 lb hydraulic table lift for motorcycles, ATVs, quads, side-by-sides and snowmobiles. Call or text Bruce at (250) 857-4665 for pricing and availability.

Will it lift an ATV or side-by-side?

Yes. The main deck is 30" wide for motorcycles, and two 15" slide-in extensions bring it to a 60" platform for ATVs, quads and side-by-sides, up to 2,200 lbs.

What power does a motorcycle lift need?

The Performance Hoist Motorcycle/ATV Lift runs on standard 110V power. No special wiring or air supply is needed.
