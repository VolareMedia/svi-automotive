A good motorcycle lift gets the bike up to a comfortable working height. You can service the chain, change tires, swap oil and detail it without kneeling on the garage floor.

Most bike lifts are table-style: you roll the motorcycle onto a platform, secure the front wheel in a vise or chock, and raise it. A drop-out panel at the rear lets you pull the back wheel without taking the bike off the lift.

CapacityMake sure it handles your heaviest bike with room to spare.
Wheel vise and locksA solid front-wheel vise and mechanical safety locks at each height.
FootprintCheck the platform length and whether it rolls out of the way when not in use.
