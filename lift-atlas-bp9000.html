Atlas BP9000

A 9,000 lb baseplate two-post lift built for low-ceiling garages, with 9' 3 1/2" columns.

The Atlas BP9000 is an economical 9,000 lb baseplate (bottom plate) two-post lift, great for low-ceiling garages. Its 111 1/2" (9' 3 1/2") columns let it install in almost any professional or home garage with a ceiling as low as 9' 6". Atlas now lists this lift under part number 9KBP.

Overview

About the Atlas BP9000

Dual hydraulic chain-drive cylinders, a Secondary Lock System and a dual-point lock release keep it simple and safe. Symmetric arms with automatic arm restraints reach down to a 4" minimum arm height, and it ships with a free 8-piece truck adapter set and mounting hardware.

It lifts to 74 1/2" (81" with adapters) and gives you 101" of drive-through between the columns. It's a step up in capacity from the Atlas BP8000 for most cars, SUVs and light trucks, though Atlas doesn't recommend it for diesel duallies or other large trucks. It needs at least 4" of 3,000 psi concrete and a 220V, 30A circuit.

Warranty Information

Every Atlas lift supplied and installed by SVI Automotive Equipment is covered by the Atlas manufacturer warranty and backed by our local service across Vancouver Island.

Standard Manufacturer Warranty

  • 5 years on structural components
  • 2 years on hydraulic cylinders
  • 1 year on electric / air-hydraulic power units
  • 90 days on wear items

Optional Extended Warranty

Atlas offers two extended-warranty programs you can add at purchase, covering parts including hydraulic hoses, fittings, pumps, and cylinders:
