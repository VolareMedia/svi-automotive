Overview

About the Atlas 12AWFSL

The in-ramp wheels-free jack system raises to 17", and the main lift reaches 84 1/4". Precise platform leveling, recessed turntables with covers and 53" approach ramps make it easy to load and align everything from small cars to one-ton dual-rear-wheel pickups.

Mechanical safety locks engage and release by air, and an emergency manual lowering pump, overload valve and lowering speed control are built in. Mounting hardware and an air line kit are included. It runs on 220V single phase with a 30A breaker and is delivered by flat deck.
