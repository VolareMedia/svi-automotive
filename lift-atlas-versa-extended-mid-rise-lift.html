Atlas Versa Extended Mid-Rise Lift

A free-standing, portable 7,000 lb mid-rise lift with a pneumatic pump, designed and made in Europe.

OverviewFeaturesSpecsWarranty

The Atlas Versa Extended is a free-standing, portable 7,000 lb mid-rise scissor lift, designed and made in Europe. It raises the vehicle to a comfortable working height of up to 39 3/8" and works in home garages or commercial shops. It's primarily geared toward quick bays: tire and brake service, detailing, wrapping and other light jobs around the wheel.

Overview

About the Atlas Versa Extended Mid-Rise Lift

Four support arms rotate 180°, and the two rear arms adjust to reach the vehicle's sills. Four support plates with adjustable-height adapters, five safety lock positions, an emergency stop switch and a foot-pedal-controlled pneumatic pump make it simple to use.

It weighs 595 lb and comes with a maneuvering drawbar, so you can roll it where you need it and store it when the bay needs to do something else. An optional caster kit (CASTER-VERSA-7MR) adds even more mobility.
