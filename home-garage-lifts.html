FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best car lift for a home garage?

It depends on your ceiling and what you want to do. For storage and light service, a four-post parking lift like the Performance Hoist PH8S or Atlas Apex 9 is hard to beat. For repairs under a lower ceiling, a baseplate two-post like the Atlas BP8000 works well. Portable mid-rise lifts suit the tightest spaces.

Can I run a car lift on 110V power?

Yes. The Performance Hoist PH8S runs on 110V with a 20A breaker, and the Atlas Apex 9 and Atlas Garage Pro 8000 run on 110V with a 30A breaker. The Atlas Kwik Bay uses a 120V power unit (30A breaker) plus shop air.

How much ceiling height do I need?

Less than you might think. The Atlas BP8000 two-post installs under ceilings as low as 9' 3", and mid-rise lifts only raise the vehicle to about 40". We measure your space before recommending anything.

Do you install lifts in home garages?

Yes. We install across Vancouver Island, from Victoria to Campbell River, and check your slab, ceiling and power first.
