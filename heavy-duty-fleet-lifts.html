FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What lift do I need for heavy trucks and fleet vehicles?

It depends on the vehicles and the work. For long-wheelbase and service trucks, an overhead two-post like the Atlas Platinum PVL-15 (15,000 lb) or Atlas OH-10X gives full underbody access. For heavy trucks and vans that need a stable drive-on platform, a four-post like the Atlas 414 or Atlas Platinum PVL-14 (14,000 lb) is a strong fit. Tell us your fleet and we'll recommend a lift.

Can I do wheel alignments on a heavy-duty lift?

Yes. The Atlas 412A and Atlas Platinum PVL14OF-EXT are four-post alignment lifts with built-in slip plates and turntables, and the Atlas 12ASL and Atlas 12AWFSL are 12,000 lb alignment scissor lifts that handle up to one-ton dual-rear-wheel pickups.

What power and floor do heavy-duty lifts need?

The heavy-duty lifts on this page run on 220V single phase with a 30A breaker, and several of the four-post and alignment lifts also need an external air supply for the lock release. Many need at least 6" of 3,000 psi concrete. We check your slab, ceiling and power before install.

Do you stock these lifts?

Some heavy-duty models are special order. Call or text Bruce at (250) 857-4665 for pricing and lead time on any Atlas heavy-duty lift.

Can I finance a heavy-duty lift?
